The Reality

Running a surgery center has never been more demanding.

Payer pressure, staffing shortages, regulatory complexity, and thin administrative bandwidth pull physicians away from patients — and put real enterprise value at risk. Most surgeons don't want to become full-time operators. They want a partner who already is one.

Three Ways to Partner

One partner. Three ways in.

Every physician and every facility is in a different place. Candor meets you where you are — from a blank parcel of land to a mature center ready for an operating partner.

Develop

Open a new ambulatory surgery center from the ground up. Our team has overseen dozens of development projects — site selection, licensure, construction, equipment, accreditation, and staffing — delivered on time and built for the procedures you actually perform.

Partner & Acquire

Bring on an aligned operating and capital partner for your existing center or practice. We structure partnerships that let you take chips off the table while keeping meaningful equity and clinical control — and position the business for long-term growth.

Operate & Manage

Keep your center; hand us the business. Full-service management across revenue cycle, payer contracting, staffing, supply chain, and compliance — measured by your margins and your case volume, not ours.
