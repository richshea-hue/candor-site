The Platform

Two MSO engines, one operating discipline.

Candor builds management services organizations around Ambulatory Surgery Centers and Pain Management practices — pairing the back-office infrastructure of an institutional operator with the alignment of a true equity partner.

Development

From ground-breaking to grand opening, we manage site selection, design, licensure, equipment planning, and construction — over 30 years of expertise standing up surgical facilities the right way.

Management

We empower physician partners to drive volume and outcomes while we mitigate the financial, regulatory, and legal risk — so clinicians can get back to the art of practicing medicine.

Revenue Cycle

End-to-end revenue management for hospital, surgical, and physician claims — billing, coding, collections, staff training, and follow-up protocols that eliminate errors and accelerate payment.

Technology

Modern systems and data infrastructure that bring transparency to operations and give physician and capital partners a clear, real-time view of facility performance.

Our Approach

Managing to bring healing and life.

Healthcare development is far more than building and running a business. At Candor, every facility serves three distinct customers — and we hold ourselves accountable to all of them.

01

Patients

Every detail of the facility is planned to deliver the highest standard of care and a patient experience that reflects well on the physicians behind it.

02

Physician Partners

We align incentives and absorb operational complexity so our partners can focus on outcomes and volume — with the upside of true ownership.

03

Community

We build real connections with the communities our facilities serve, establishing a patient- and community-centric focus that endures.
