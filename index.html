What We Do

From one practice to a multi-site platform.

Candor develops, acquires, operates, and advises surgery centers and specialty practices. You get the systems and buying power of a large operator, and a partner who holds equity in the outcome alongside you.

Surgery Center Development & Operations

Ambulatory surgery centers and surgical hospitals taken from ground-up development through licensure, staffing, and full day-to-day operations.

Practice Management

Multi-specialty physician practice management — the full MSO back office of billing, HR, compliance, payor contracting, and technology — so clinicians across any specialty can return to the art of practicing medicine.

Development & Construction

Turnkey delivery of new facilities — site selection, design-build, capital-partnership structuring, equipment planning, and construction management under one roof.

Revenue Cycle Management

End-to-end revenue management for hospital, surgical, and physician claims — billing, coding, collections, and follow-up protocols that eliminate errors and accelerate payment.

Consultative Services

Feasibility studies, pro formas, operational assessments, and turnaround advisory for physician groups, health systems, and capital partners evaluating or repositioning a facility.

Technology & Data

Modern systems and data infrastructure that bring transparency to operations and give physician and capital partners a clear, real-time view of performance.

Our Approach

Managing to bring healing and life.

Healthcare development is far more than building and running a business. At Candor, every facility serves three distinct customers — and we hold ourselves accountable to all of them.

01

Patients

Every detail of the facility is planned to deliver the highest standard of care and a patient experience that reflects well on the physicians behind it.

02

Physician Partners

We align incentives and absorb operational complexity so our partners can focus on outcomes and volume — with the upside of true ownership.

03

Community

We build real connections with the communities our facilities serve, establishing a patient- and community-centric focus that endures.
